Get to Know Us Better

InAmigos Foundation was founded on September 23, 2020, with a grassroots vision to unite compassionate citizens for social good. Registered under the Central Government with 80G, 12A, CSR-1, and NITI Aayog Darpan credentials, we ensure complete transparency and accountability for every rupee entrusted to us.

Through our six flagship initiatives — Project Sewa (hunger relief), Bachpanshaala (child education), Project Jeev (stray animal welfare), Project Udaan (women empowerment), Project Prakriti (environmental conservation), and Project Vikas (skill development) — we transform empathy into action.
